**Executive Summary**

This paper addresses the critical issue of privacy breaches in cloud data storage, proposing a novel layered framework that integrates onion and garlic privacy preserving approaches to secure user data. The framework aims to prevent digital data loss and maintain confidentiality by employing multiple encryption layers. The onion privacy preserving approach (OPPA) is introduced as a foundational layer that encrypts user data and directs requests through a series of nodes, ensuring anonymity and secure data transmission. The garlic privacy preserving approach (GPPA) is then layered on top of OPPA, enhancing security by encrypting multiple messages together, thereby complicating attacks and unauthorized access.

The proposed framework is designed to operate on the cloud layer, where user data is encrypted using public key cryptography. This approach ensures that user data remains confidential and inaccessible to unauthorized parties. The layered security model provides robust protection against privacy breaches, leveraging both onion and garlic privacy techniques to safeguard user information. The paper emphasizes the importance of these privacy preserving measures in mitigating the risks associated with cloud data storage, particularly in the context of emerging quantum threats and the need for scalable, efficient privacy-preserving solutions.

The work concludes by highlighting the necessity of adopting such frameworks for future decentralized systems, ensuring compliance with regulations like GDPR while maintaining the utility of aggregated data. Future research directions include exploring three-factor encryption and hardware-level integration to further enhance the security and efficiency of the proposed approach.
