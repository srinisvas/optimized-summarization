**Executive Summary**

The paper addresses the ethical considerations of integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Autonomous and Intelligent Systems (A/IS), particularly in the context of the Internet of Things (IoT). It emphasizes the need for open dialogue with society to ensure the responsible development and deployment of AI-driven IoT technologies. The authors advocate for adherence to the IEEE Ethically Aligned Design (EAD) principles, which provide a framework for ethical AI development. These principles encompass anthropological, political, and technical aspects, ensuring that AI systems respect human rights, are accountable, and are designed to protect natural resources and the environment. The paper highlights the importance of transparency in certification systems and the necessity of educating the public on data security and privacy. It also discusses the potential societal impacts of AI, such as changes in quality of life and professional careers, and the need for AI systems to be designed with human well-being in mind. The work concludes by proposing a process for identifying key issues and recommendations related to AI design, underscoring the importance of proactive ethical planning to avoid misuses and ensure public trust and safety.
