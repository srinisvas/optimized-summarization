**Executive Summary**

This paper addresses the critical challenge of protecting privacy in AI-powered medical systems, a field where sensitive patient data is extensively used. The authors propose a novel technique that integrates advanced privacy-preserving methods with AI performance optimization. The proposed solution employs federated learning and blockchain to securely exchange data across different organizations, ensuring privacy while enhancing AI capabilities.

The core of the approach involves using Laplace distribution noise to manage privacy budgets, which dynamically adjusts to balance data utility and individual privacy contributions. This method is complemented by regularization techniques such as stochastic gradient descent, which enhance the model's resilience to noise and ensure robust performance. The framework also incorporates iterative feedback, allowing the model to adapt and improve based on real-world data, thereby refining its diagnostic capabilities.

Key to the solution is the privacy budget, which dynamically adjusts to maintain a balance between data utility and privacy. This approach not only ensures compliance with privacy regulations like GDPR and HIPAA but also improves the overall performance of AI-driven medical systems. The use of homomorphic cryptography for data transmission across different healthcare entities is highlighted as a particularly effective method for anonymizing data, which can lead to immediate access to useful data (92% of the time), making it a practical solution for fast data access.

The proposed framework demonstrates significant improvements in various metrics, including accuracy, precision, memory, F1 score, and privacy compliance. It also enhances data, models, training, and inference speeds, making it suitable for real-time healthcare applications. The use of a privacy audit ensures that the model adheres to privacy regulations, while the AUC-ROC curve shows the reliability of the technique. The global model developed through this approach is ready for deployment, providing a balanced solution that combines excellent analytical abilities with rigorous privacy precautions.

The work's primary contribution is a scalable and adaptable privacy-protected model that can adapt to the evolving demands of healthcare. This new approach not only meets the stringent privacy requirements but also improves patient outcomes and decision-making. By integrating privacy-preserving techniques with AI performance optimization, the proposed framework sets a new standard for the privacy and data usage in AI-powered medical systems, offering a pathway for regulatory compliance without compromising the analytical value of aggregated data.
