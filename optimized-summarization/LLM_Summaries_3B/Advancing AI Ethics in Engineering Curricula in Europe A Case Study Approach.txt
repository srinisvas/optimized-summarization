**Executive Summary**

The Erasmus+ Ethical Engineer project aims to integrate ethical, social, and legal aspects into engineering curricula, promoting truthful AI in Europe. The paper presents a structured approach to creating AI ethics case studies, which are essential for fostering ethical awareness among students and professionals. Through a series of case studies, the project explores various ethical dilemmas arising from AI applications in different domains, such as healthcare, witness testimonies, facial recognition, self-driving taxis, and ecological impact. The case studies are designed to cover a broad range of real-life scenarios, ensuring a comprehensive understanding of the ethical challenges associated with AI.

The methodology involves a combination of group discussions, role-playing, reflective writing, and guest speaker interactions, which are tailored to enhance critical thinking and ethical decision-making. The primary contribution of the project is the development of a structured template for creating these case studies, which has been refined based on feedback from educators and students. The template ensures that each case study is well-structured and addresses key ethical concerns, such as patient safety, informed consent, and regulatory adherence.

The case studies also delve into the ecological impact of AI, the potential for bias in data, and the ethical implications of AI in marketing and witness testimonies. The project emphasizes the importance of balancing AI's benefits with its potential risks, such as job displacement and the generation of fake content. By examining these diverse scenarios, the case studies provide a practical framework for educators to engage students in ethical discussions and encourage responsible AI development.

The project's findings underscore the necessity of ethical considerations in AI education, highlighting the need for a multidisciplinary approach that combines technical knowledge with ethical awareness. The structured template developed by the project serves as a valuable resource for educators aiming to enhance their students' understanding of AI ethics. By addressing a wide range of ethical issues, the case studies contribute to a more informed and responsible AI ecosystem, aligning with global standards like the GDPR. Future work will focus on validating the effectiveness of the template and exploring its integration into broader educational frameworks.
