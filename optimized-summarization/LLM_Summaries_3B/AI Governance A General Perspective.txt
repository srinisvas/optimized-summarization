**Executive Summary**

This paper addresses the urgent topic of artificial intelligence (AI) governance, emphasizing the need for frameworks that ensure ethical and responsible use of AI across various sectors. The authors highlight the risks associated with AI, including privacy infringement, bias, discrimination, and unemployment, and argue that these risks exacerbate existing inequalities. They discuss various international initiatives aimed at AI governance, such as the Shanghai Declaration on Global AI Governance, the IMF Framework for AI Governance, UNESCO's AI Ethics Recommendation, the OECD AI Principles, and the European AI Act. These initiatives underscore key principles of AI governance, including accountability, transparency, integrity, and fairness.

The paper advocates for the development of AI governance frameworks that promote fairness and protect individual rights. It calls for international cooperation, with a recommendation to host an international conference under the auspices of the United Nations to develop and refine AI governance rules and frameworks. The authors also emphasize the importance of continuous monitoring and evaluation of AI systems to identify and correct bias.

The paper concludes by advocating for the creation of transparent and explainable AI systems, ensuring that data, algorithms, capabilities, and purposes are clearly defined and accessible to those affected by AI. It acknowledges the rapid pace of AI development and the need for adaptable governance frameworks to keep pace with technological advancements. The authors also note the challenges posed by AI systems, such as their potential to exacerbate climate degradation and threaten human rights, and call for a balanced approach to AI governance that prioritizes ethical considerations and human rights.

The paper concludes by advocating for the development of adaptable AI governance frameworks that can evolve with the technology, ensuring that they remain relevant and effective in addressing the ethical and societal challenges posed by AI. It calls for a collaborative approach, involving all stakeholders, to create a framework that promotes fairness, transparency, and accountability in AI governance.
