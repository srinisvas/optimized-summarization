**Executive Summary**

The Center for Standards and Ethics in Artificial Intelligence (CSEAI) is a collaborative initiative aimed at promoting safe, effective, and ethical AI standards through research, outreach, and education in partnership with industry and government. The CSEAI's primary objectives include fostering a diverse workforce of students and professionals, advancing research in AI protocols and technologies, and facilitating the adoption of standardized, ethical AI products that are safe for users and promote diversity in the field.

The CSEAI's workforce development plan emphasizes mentoring undergraduate and graduate students, as well as providing continuing education for industry professionals. This initiative is designed to ensure a diverse and well-trained workforce capable of developing and implementing responsible AI technologies. The center's research focuses on critical areas such as bias mitigation, fairness evaluation, and product safety through AI-guided adversarial attacks, which are essential for creating trustworthy AI products and services.

The CSEAI's efforts align with the Ethics in the Global Innovation Helix conference theme by promoting ethical practices in AI. The center's initiatives, including the production of standardized, ethical AI products, the recruitment and training of a diverse workforce, and the advancement of research in AI protocols and technologies, demonstrate a strong commitment to socially responsible innovation. These actions are crucial for increasing trust in AI products and services and ensuring regulatory compliance, such as adhering to the GDPR.

The CSEAI's partnership with Baylor University, Rutgers University, and the University of Miami underscores its interdisciplinary approach and broad impact. The center's research and educational programs are designed to address the pressing need for socially responsible innovation in AI, particularly in industries where AI-enabled technologies are increasingly prevalent. By focusing on the development of safe, effective, and ethical AI standards, the CSEAI is contributing significantly to the advancement of responsible AI practices and the protection of consumers.

The CSEAI's work is supported by the NSF under grant CNS-2136961, highlighting the importance of the center's mission and the potential impact of its initiatives on the global AI landscape. Overall, the CSEAI represents a vital step towards fostering a culture of ethical AI practices and promoting socially responsible innovation, which is essential for the future of AI technologies.
