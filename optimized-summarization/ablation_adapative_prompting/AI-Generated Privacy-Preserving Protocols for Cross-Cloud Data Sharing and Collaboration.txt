**Executive Summary**

This paper introduces AI-generated privacy-preserving protocols for cross-cloud data sharing and collaboration, addressing the challenges of heterogeneous cloud environments and the need for robust privacy protection. The proposed solution integrates federated learning, differential privacy, dynamic encryption, and context-aware policies to secure data sharing while maintaining high performance. The architecture includes a Data Ingestion Layer, an AI Processing Module, a Secured Data Sharing Layer, and a Cross-Cloud Collaboration Layer, each contributing to privacy, security, and performance. The system is evaluated using metrics such as privacy scores, attack resistance, latency, and resource utilization, demonstrating superior performance compared to existing protocols like SMC and ABE. The use of AI-driven methods ensures adaptability to evolving threats and regulatory compliance, making it a promising solution for industries such as healthcare and finance. Future work includes optimizing resource management, improving model aggregation, and enhancing quantum-resistant protocols, as well as exploring the integration of zero-knowledge proofs and blockchain for further trust and transparency.
