**Executive Summary**

The Erasmus+ Ethical Engineer project aims to integrate ethical, social, and legal aspects into engineering curricula, promoting truthful AI in Europe. The paper presents a structured approach for developing case studies that address the ethical implications of AI in various domains. Key case studies include AI in healthcare, witness testimonies, facial recognition, self-driving taxis, ecological impact of generative AI, AI in marketing, AI in assessing historic photos, continuous artificial hearts, data analytics, and bias in data.

The project emphasizes the importance of ethical instruction in engineering education, particularly in the context of artificial intelligence. It outlines objectives for ethical instruction, such as stimulating ethical imagination, teaching ethical analysis, and fostering ethical decision-making. The case studies are designed to engage students in critical thinking and ethical reasoning, covering diverse applications and ethical dilemmas.

The structured template for case studies includes an introduction to the scenario, background information, narrative section with real-life examples, ethical considerations, open-ended questions, learning objectives, and teaching recommendations. The case studies are developed to reflect the diversity of AI applications and their ethical challenges, such as privacy risks, data security, and algorithmic bias.

The paper concludes by advocating for the adoption of AI ethics case studies in engineering education to prepare students for the complex ethical implications of AI in their future careers. By integrating these case studies into curricula, educators can equip students with the skills to navigate ethical dilemmas and uphold professional integrity in the rapidly evolving field of artificial intelligence.
