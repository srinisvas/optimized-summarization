**Executive Summary**

The IEEE Global Initiative on Ethics of Extended Reality (XR) -XR- Report--Extended Reality -XR- Ethics in Medicine presents an ethical framework for the responsible development and use of XR technology in medical applications. XR, which includes Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), and Mixed Reality (MR), is transforming healthcare by enhancing patient care, medical education, and research. The report emphasizes the need for a comprehensive ethical approach to address the unique challenges and risks associated with XR in medicine.

Key ethical principles guiding the use of XR in medicine include respect for autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, and justice. These principles are essential for ensuring that XR applications are developed and used in a manner that respects patient rights and promotes the well-being of individuals. The report highlights the importance of informed consent, particularly in scenarios where XR can alter perceptions and behaviors, and the need for transparent data collection and management practices.

The report also discusses several ethical issues and risks associated with XR in medicine, such as privacy and security concerns, potential for distraction and disorientation, and the risk of accidents or injury. It advocates for the establishment of Centers of Excellence to regulate and oversee the development and validation of XR tools, as well as to ensure equal accessibility to these technologies. Additionally, the report calls for the adoption of guidelines and decision-making models that integrate ethical considerations into the design and use of XR applications.

The report concludes by emphasizing the urgent need for a structured ethical approach to XR in medicine, one that addresses the unique challenges posed by this emerging technology and ensures its responsible integration into healthcare. It underscores the importance of collaboration between stakeholders, including academia, healthcare providers, and XR device manufacturers, to advance the field while safeguarding patient interests and promoting equitable access to XR-enabled medical solutions.
