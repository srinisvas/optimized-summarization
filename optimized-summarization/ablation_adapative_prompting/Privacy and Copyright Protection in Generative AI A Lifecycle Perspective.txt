**Executive Summary**

The advent of Generative AI (GenAI) has revolutionized the field of artificial intelligence, enabling the creation of realistic images, texts, and data patterns. However, this advancement has also raised significant concerns over data privacy and copyright infringement, primarily due to the reliance on vast datasets for model training. Traditional approaches to privacy and copyright protection, such as differential privacy, machine unlearning, and data poisoning, have been found to be insufficient for addressing the complex issues arising from GenAI.

This paper examines the multifaceted challenges of privacy and copyright protection within the data lifecycle of GenAI, advocating for integrated approaches that combine technical innovation with ethical foresight. The research highlights the need for comprehensive solutions that address privacy and copyright concerns across the entire data lifecycle, ensuring both data utility and legal compliance.

Key challenges identified include consent and license acquisition, transparency and data access, consent modification and withdrawal, provenance and attribution, ide effects, continuous monitoring of privacy breaches and copyright violations, downstream distribution, and the necessity of lifecycle approaches. These challenges are interconnected and span multiple stages of the data lifecycle, from data collection and annotation to model deployment and inference.

To address these challenges, the paper proposes several directions for future research. These include developing comprehensive consent management systems, improving machine forgetting techniques to mitigate side effects, establishing reliable guardrails for privacy and copyright protection, creating AI Bill of Materials for compliance, and integrating these measures throughout the entire data lifecycle. By adopting a lifecycle perspective, the research aims to foster a broader discussion and inspire concerted efforts towards data privacy and copyright integrity in GenAI, ensuring that these critical issues are addressed holistically and effectively.
