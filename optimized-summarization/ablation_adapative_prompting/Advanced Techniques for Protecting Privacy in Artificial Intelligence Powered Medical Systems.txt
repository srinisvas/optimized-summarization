**Title: Advanced Techniques for Protecting Privacy in Artificial Intelligence Powered Medical Systems**

This paper presents a novel framework for securing patient data in AI-powered medical systems while maintaining the analytical value of the data. The proposed method integrates differential privacy (DP), homomorphic encryption, and federated learning to address privacy concerns in healthcare data. The framework employs a privacy budget to manage noise levels, ensuring individual data contributions remain private. It also utilizes Laplace distribution noise to protect data integrity and incorporates iterative feedback to refine model parameters based on real-world data.

Key contributions of the research include the development of a quantum-resistant differential privacy (QRDP) framework that significantly reduces computational overhead, improving query response time by 35% compared to classic DP models. The QRDP framework combines homomorphic encryption for secure data processing and a specialized noise injection protocol guided by machine learning optimization. The work demonstrates the feasibility of balancing quantum-level security with necessary data utility for IoT applications, offering a pathway for regulatory compliance without sacrificing data utility.

The proposed methodology ensures privacy compliance by conducting full privacy checks and adhering to legal standards. The framework's ability to adapt to healthcare demands and provide a true, scalable solution makes it a new standard for AI app privacy and data usage. The technology enhances decision-making and patient outcomes by enabling AI in healthcare, while maintaining robust privacy protections.

The results of the proposed technique show superior performance in accuracy, precision, memory, F1 score, and privacy compliance compared to existing approaches, making it a reliable and effective solution for AI-driven medical analytics. The framework's performance in various healthcare scenarios, including prompt response times, highlights its practicality and value in sensitive data environments. The work provides a comprehensive approach to privacy protection in AI-powered medical systems, addressing the critical need for both data security and analytical utility.
