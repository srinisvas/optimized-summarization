A mixed-methods approach, combining surveys and qualitative analysis, was employed to examine five key constructs: Data Ownership and Control (DOC), Parental Data Sharing (PDS), Perceived Risks and Benefits (PRB), Transparency and Trust (TT), and Education and Awareness (EA).
