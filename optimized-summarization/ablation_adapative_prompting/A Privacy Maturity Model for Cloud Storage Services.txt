**Executive Summary**

This paper presents a Privacy Maturity Model (PMM) for Cloud Storage Services, aiming to assess the level of privacy afforded by these services. The model is based on the CMMI-SVC framework and the BSIMM-CP model, focusing on compliance policies and governance related to Personally Identifiable Information (PII). The authors emphasize the lack of a universal consensus on what constitutes sensitive data, highlighting the need for a comprehensive approach to privacy management throughout the data lifecycle.

The PMM addresses key concerns such as data sensitivity identification, protection strategies, and the establishment of privacy levels that meet user needs. The model seeks to bridge the gap between current privacy practices and the evolving needs of cloud storage services, particularly in the context of cloud computing's scalability and heterogeneity.

The proposed solution includes a four-phase methodology: planning and systematic review, development of the solution, evaluation of the solution, and publication of results. This approach ensures a structured and comprehensive evaluation of the proposed techniques, with a focus on metrics and experiments to validate the effectiveness of the privacy measures.

The work concludes by advocating for the adoption of the PMM to enhance privacy in cloud storage services, aligning with regulatory requirements and ensuring user trust. Future research will focus on hardware-level integration and real-world validation to further strengthen the framework's resilience against quantum threats and data loss scenarios.
