**Executive Summary**

This paper addresses the ethical and operational challenges known as Values Debt in Generative AI (GenAI), which arise from insufficient ethical considerations during development. The Helpful, Honest, Harmless (HHH) framework is introduced as a strategy to align AI systems with human values. A case study of GRAISE, a Graph RAG-based chatbot for aviation safety, demonstrates how the HHH framework can mitigate Values Debt and enhance the ethical integrity of AI applications.

GRAISE integrates GPT-4 with a graph-structured aviation knowledge base, ensuring that its outputs are grounded in validated domain data. This approach minimizes hallucinations and misinformation, aligning with the HHH framework's principles of being Helpful, Honest, and Harmless. The HHH framework ensures that GRAISE provides accurate, contextually relevant responses that support pilot training while avoiding harmful or misleading advice.

The HHH framework is applied throughout the development lifecycle of GRAISE, from requirements engineering and data management to deployment and ongoing monitoring. This holistic approach ensures that GRAISE's design prioritizes fairness, accountability, and transparency, thereby enhancing user trust and safety in high-stakes environments like aviation.

The paper also explores the broader implications of the HHH framework and Graph RAG approach in other domains. By integrating domain-specific knowledge and ensuring transparency and verifiability, AI systems can enhance accuracy, explainability, and fairness. This strategy can be applied to various sectors, such as finance, education, and environmental conservation, to improve ethical performance and build public trust in AI technologies.

The GRAISE case study highlights the effectiveness of the proposed strategies in addressing Values Debt and promoting responsible AI engineering. By embedding ethical considerations from the outset and continuously evaluating AI systems, organizations can create AI applications that are both technically robust and socially responsible. This proactive approach not only enhances the ethical integrity of AI systems but also fosters a collaborative relationship between humans and AI, shaping each other for mutual benefit.
