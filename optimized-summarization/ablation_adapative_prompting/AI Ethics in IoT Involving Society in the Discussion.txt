**Executive Summary**

The paper addresses the ethical considerations surrounding the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Autonomous and Intelligent Systems (A/IS) into the Internet of Things (IoT), emphasizing the need for open dialogue with society to ensure the responsible development and deployment of these technologies. The authors argue that the recent surge in AI applications, such as OpenAI ChatGPT and Google Bard, has raised concerns about their impact on education, fraud, and job displacement, necessitating a proactive approach to ethical AI design.

The paper introduces the IEEE Global Initiative on Autonomous and Intelligent Systems (A/IS) Ethics, which provides a framework for ensuring AI systems align with human values and ethical principles. The framework is divided into three pillars: Universal Human Values, Political Self-Determination and Data Agency, and Technical Dependability. These pillars are further elaborated into eight general principles, including human rights, well-being, data agency, effectiveness, transparency, accountability, awareness of misuse, and competence.

The authors highlight the importance of adhering to these principles to foster trust and acceptance of AI and IoT technologies among the public. They advocate for a collaborative and inclusive approach to AI development, involving stakeholders from various disciplines and sectors. The paper underscores the need for continuous reflection and discussion within society to ensure that AI and IoT projects are guided by ethical considerations and contribute positively to human well-being and environmental sustainability.

In conclusion, the paper calls for a proactive and participatory approach to AI ethics, advocating for the adoption of the IEEE EAD principles to guide the design, development, and implementation of AI and IoT technologies. By fostering open dialogue and adherence to ethical guidelines, the paper aims to mitigate potential risks and ensure that AI and IoT advancements benefit society as a whole.
