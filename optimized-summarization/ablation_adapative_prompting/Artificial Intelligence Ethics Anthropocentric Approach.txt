**Executive Summary**

This paper critically examines the ethical implications of artificial intelligence (AI) through an anthropocentric approach, addressing concerns about the uncanny valley effect and alarmist human-centered approaches. The authors argue that an anthropocentric perspective, which focuses on human-centric moral considerations, provides a more nuanced understanding of AI ethics. This approach allows for the analysis of both subject-object moral relationships and the exceptions to these principles, thereby enabling a more comprehensive description of AI ethics. The paper highlights the need to balance human-centric values with the technical and societal challenges of AI development, advocating for an anthropocentric framework that can better navigate the complexities of AI ethics in an increasingly interconnected world.
