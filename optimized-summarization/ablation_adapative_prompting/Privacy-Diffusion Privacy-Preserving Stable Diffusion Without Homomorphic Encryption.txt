**Executive Summary**

This paper introduces Privacy-Diffusion, a novel privacy-preserving framework for text-to-image generation that addresses the critical challenge of protecting user privacy without the computational overhead and potential quality degradation associated with fully homomorphic encryption (FHE). The framework leverages the irreversible properties of neural network layers and the normalized Gaussian distribution of predicted noise in the diffusion process to ensure privacy without the need for FHE or differential privacy techniques. 

Key contributions of the work include the development of a protocol that maintains high-quality image output while achieving the same security level as FHE-based methods with significantly reduced computational time. The authors demonstrate that their protocol can achieve this balance with only a 4% increase in processing time compared to FHE-based approaches, which previously required 200% more time and resulted in visible quality loss. This represents a significant advancement in privacy-preserving diffusion models, offering a practical solution for protecting user input text prompts, output images, and customized models in generative AI applications.

The work builds upon the foundational concepts of Stable Diffusion, a leading open-source text-to-image generation model, and integrates privacy-preserving techniques to ensure robust security without compromising the efficiency and quality of the generated images. The proposed framework is designed to be scalable and adaptable to various generative AI models, with potential applications in both commercial and research settings. By providing a clear and efficient method for maintaining privacy, Privacy-Diffusion contributes to the growing interest in privacy-preserving technologies in the field of generative AI.
