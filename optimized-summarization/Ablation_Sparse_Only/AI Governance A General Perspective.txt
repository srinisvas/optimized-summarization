is research paper discusses the governance of artificial intelligence, highlighting the risks and challenges associated with its use in various sectors such as healthcare, education, research, business, transportation, and more. It concludes by advocating for international collaboration, particularly through a United Nations conference involving major technology companies, to establish effective AI governance frameworks. The paper also calls for further research in this area, including specialized studies on specific frameworks and addressing challenges like rapid technological changes and the need for adaptable regulations. Key phrases include artificial intelligence, governance of artificial intelligence, AI applications, privacy, bias, and international initiatives.
