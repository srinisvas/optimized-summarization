The research paper discusses the ethical considerations and general principles of IEEE Ethically Aligned Design (EAD) in the context of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies. It emphasizes the importance of transparency and accountability in AI development and implementation, particularly in addressing concerns about AI chatbots and their potential impact on education, fraud, and professions. The paper highlights the need for public discussion and support for AI and IoT projects, guided by the IEEE EAD framework, to prevent unfounded rumors and ensure the responsible advancement of these technologies. Key points include the protection of human rights, environmental sustainability, and the development of necessary skills for AI operators. The paper also addresses the importance of awareness of misuse and the role of certification systems in promoting transparency. Overall, the research underscores the necessity of ethical AI design to foster trust, ensure public benefit, and mitigate potential risks associated with AI and IoT technologies.
