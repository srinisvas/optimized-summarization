The CSEAI, led by Baylor University and partnered with Rutgers University and the University of Miami, aims to promote socially responsible innovation through education, outreach, and research in AI. The center's initiatives include workforce development plans that involve mentoring undergraduates, graduate students, and postdocs, as well as providing continuing education for industry professionals. By advancing research in AI protocols, procedures, and technologies, and focusing on bias mitigation, fairness evaluation, and product safety, the CSEAI demonstrates its commitment to responsible, equitable, traceable, reliable, and governable development of AI-fueled technologies. The center's efforts align with the Ethics in the Global Innovation Helix conference theme by promoting ethical practices in AI and increasing trustworthiness in AI products and services. The CSEAI's initiatives are supported by the NSF under grant CNS-2136961, and various regulatory groups have recently produced critical standards for AI ethics, with the number of standards currently in production being unprecedented.
