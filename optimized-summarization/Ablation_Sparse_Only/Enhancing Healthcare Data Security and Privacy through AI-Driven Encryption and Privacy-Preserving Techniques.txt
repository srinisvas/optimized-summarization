The research paper discusses the integration of AI with traditional encryption techniques to create a dynamic encryption environment that adapts to the sensitivity of data and the access level of users. The approach incorporates privacy-preserving methodologies such as differential privacy, federated learning, and homomorphic encryption to enable secure collaboration in analyzing and researching patient data while maintaining privacy. The methodology also uses AI for intrusion detection and compliance management, ensuring sustained compliance and security, and adherence to legal frameworks like HIPAA and GDPR. The evaluation results show that the proposed AI-based encryption achieves better security outcomes compared to traditional methods, reduces privacy threats and false alarm violations, and enhances system performance. The approach automates compliance tasks, reducing the workload for healthcare organizations and minimizing errors. Future work includes enhancing the proposed AI encryption framework to incorporate quantum-safe encryption algorithms, modern threat intelligence systems, and federated learning models to improve extensibility for large health-care networks. Additionally, it involves developing energy-efficient encryption algorithms, lighter AI models, and testing the model in real healthcare systems to establish scalability. The methodology also addresses cross-border data sharing scenarios and international regulatory compliance, supporting international clinical research cooperation. Overall, the research paper presents a robust, scalable, and effective solution for protecting healthcare data while maintaining privacy and enabling efficient data processing.
