The Erasmus+ Ethical Engineer project explores AI ethics through various case studies, including AI in healthcare, witness testimonies, facial recognition, self-driving taxis, generative AI, marketing, and assessing historic photos. The paper presents a structured template for creating AI ethics case studies and discusses the importance of ethical considerations in AI education. The project aims to enhance AI education by integrating ethical, social, and legal aspects into engineering training, promoting truthful AI in Europe. The case studies cover a wide range of areas, from medical devices to marketing and beyond, highlighting the need for transparency, accountability, and the avoidance of bias in AI systems. The project also emphasizes the importance of addressing ethical challenges and promoting responsible AI development. The main contribution of the paper is the development of a structured template for creating AI ethics case studies, which has been refined based on feedback from teachers and students. The template aims to cover a larger variety of examples from different areas of real life, ensuring that students can engage with a wide range of ethical dilemmas and develop critical thinking skills. The project's methodology includes group discussions, role-playing, and reflective writing, as well as discussions with industry experts and ethicists. The case studies are designed to be interactive and engaging, with characters and roles introduced to personalize the ethical dilemmas.
