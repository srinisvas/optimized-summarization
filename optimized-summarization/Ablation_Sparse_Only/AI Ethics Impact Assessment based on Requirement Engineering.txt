The research paper presents a methodology for evaluating the ethical impact of AI systems based on AI ethics guidelines. The authors analyzed past ethical issues with AI and assumed that ethical risks are associated with interactions between AI systems and stakeholders. They developed an AI ethics model that embodies ethics guidelines and associates them with interactions using requirement engineering, and an impact assessment process. The methodology was validated through a questionnaire survey, which suggested the validity of the assumption but had limitations due to a small number of participants and bias towards those with knowledge of AI ethics. The authors propose the AI Ethics Impact Assessment as a comprehensive framework for extracting potential ethical risks in AI systems in accordance with AI ethics guidelines. The methodology involves constructing an AI ethics model that correlates ethics guidelines with interactions and an impact assessment process that uses the AI ethics model to extract ethical risks for individual AI systems. The approach consists of three steps: (1) analyzing the AI system's components and stakeholders, (2) constructing an AI ethics model that correlates ethics guidelines with interactions, and (3) conducting an impact assessment using the AI ethics model. The methodology can be used by AI developers, providers, and users without expertise in ethical guidelines to assess ethical risks at each stage of the AI lifecycle, from planning to development, operation, and retirement. The authors also discuss the limitations of the methodology and suggest future directions for improvement, such as expanding the validation method and involving a wider range of participants.
