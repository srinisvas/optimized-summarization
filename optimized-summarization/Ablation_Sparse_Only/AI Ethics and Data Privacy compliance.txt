The research paper discusses the impact of technological evolution on society, focusing on the role of robots in the economy and the protection of citizens' lives. It highlights the issues of misinformation, manipulation, and fake news in digital media systems. The paper also covers the legislative and normative documents promoting citizens' rights and freedoms in the EU. The key phrases include artificial intelligence, data, information, GDPR, AI Partnership for Defense, and user rights. The main objectives of information and documentation systems are analyzed, with common issues such as lodging complaints with the Supervisory Authority and the need for democratic access to cultural information storages. The main objectives of the information and documentation systems are to lodge a complaint with the Supervisory Authority, and to have democratic access to cultural information storages in national and international digital collections, without discrimination. The paper also discusses the challenges in implementing data protection measures, such as deleting data concerning the user, which can be difficult procedures.
