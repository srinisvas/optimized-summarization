The research paper discusses the ethical implications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in healthcare, highlighting the need for ethical introspection and governance. It emphasizes the importance of informed consent, data privacy, and continuous monitoring of AI systems. The paper advocates for a balanced approach between AI and human oversight, addressing challenges such as bias, emotional intelligence, and the need for diverse stakeholder involvement. It also explores the integration of AI in healthcare, the risks of ignoring ethical pitfalls, and the necessity of contextually sensitive approaches. The paper calls for transparency, accountability, and fairness in AI algorithms, as well as the promotion of ethical practices and the elimination of discrimination. It underscores the importance of human intervention in critical situations and the need for ongoing evaluation and adaptation of AI systems to ensure trust and security in healthcare. The research also acknowledges the limitations of relying solely on academic literature and the potential exclusion of other significant sources of information. Overall, the paper aims to guide the development of ethically sound AI applications in healthcare, benefiting both patients and practitioners.
