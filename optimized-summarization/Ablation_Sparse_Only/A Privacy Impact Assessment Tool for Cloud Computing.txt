The research paper discusses the development of a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) decision support tool designed for integration within a cloud computing environment. The tool aims to evaluate privacy risks and compliance issues associated with cloud services, helping decision-makers assess the potential effects of new projects on individuals' privacy. The PIA tool utilizes a rule-based system to structure privacy assessments, offering facilities for secure cryptographic key generation and limiting their use. It also incorporates a hardware pseudo-random number generator to enhance security. The tool is capable of storing sensitive data on its own data center while utilizing public and private cloud services for infrastructure and computing. The PIA tool addresses the complexity of privacy compliance requirements by highlighting privacy risks and compliance issues, allowing non-experts to identify solutions in specific situations. The tool employs a color-coded system to convey risk levels associated with different categories, with black indicating high risk, grey indicating a warning, and white indicating low risk. The paper outlines the structure of the PIA tool, its integration within cloud computing environments, and its benefits in raising the privacy baseline of systems without significant impact on functionality. The research emphasizes the importance of PIAs in facilitating proactive privacy management and their increasing relevance as cloud service adoption grows. The paper also briefly mentions two Decision Support Systems (DSSs) that are at the forefront of research. Overall, the PIA tool is designed to be a valuable resource for organizations seeking to ensure privacy compliance and mitigate potential risks in their cloud computing environments.
