e research paper proposes an integrated approach combining Federated Learning (FL), Explainable AI (XAI), and deep learning to enhance brain tumor detection by MRI scans. The proposed system achieves high accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, and F1-score, outperforming traditional CNN models. However, it faces challenges such as computational cost, real-time processing, and interpretability. The future direction includes optimizing FL for faster aggregation, improving model robustness with diverse datasets, and enhancing XAI for faster explanation generation. The system aims to improve privacy, trust, and efficiency in AI-driven brain tumor diagnosis, bridging AI research to clinical applications.
