The research paper discusses the importance of privacy and data management in cloud computing, addressing the concerns of clients about data privacy when outsourcing to other companies. It proposes an intelligent cloud system that utilizes a Privacy Manager and Risk Manager to ensure data integrity and user privacy. The system employs encryption in the Privacy Manager to secure data before transmission to the cloud database. The paper also covers the architecture of the proposed system, which can be implemented using Eucalyptus cloud environment and Aneka tool. Additionally, it highlights the role of digital certificates in public-key encryption and the benefits of using separate logins for different services like e-mail, Facebook, Twitter, and cloud storage. The paper emphasizes the need for cloud providers to find alternative methods to protect client privacy information and the importance of addressing privacy issues in the cloud environment. The research also touches on the concept of cloud computing, its different dimensions, and the challenges of mapping data across different environments. Overall, the paper aims to provide a comprehensive solution for ensuring privacy and data management in cloud computing.
