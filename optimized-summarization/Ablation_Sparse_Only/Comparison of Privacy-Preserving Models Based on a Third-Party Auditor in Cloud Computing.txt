This research paper explores the role of third-party auditors (TPAs) in securing cloud computing environments, focusing on privacy-preserving models and their limitations. The paper compares existing TPA-based approaches, classifies them based on security methods, and evaluates their impact on cloud data privacy. It highlights the challenges posed by TPAs, such as trust issues and potential data manipulation breaches. The study also presents a novel lightweight solution, LAPP, which aims to alleviate the gap in cloud users' decision-making and increase trust in TPA-based solutions. The paper concludes by recommending future research directions for developing more secure and efficient privacy-preserving models. Key phrases include cloud computing, data privacy, third-party auditors, privacy-preserving models, and their limitations. The summary emphasizes the importance of addressing trust concerns and managing potential vulnerabilities in cloud environments.
