research paper discusses the development of a Privacy Maturity Model for cloud storage services, focusing on the challenges and barriers in ensuring privacy within the cloud computing environment. The authors present a four-phase methodology to address privacy concerns, including the identification of sensitive data, the formulation of compliance policies, and the implementation of privacy measures. They also highlight the importance of privacy in cloud computing, referencing existing frameworks such as HIPAA and BSIMM-CP, and emphasize the need for a comprehensive approach to privacy management throughout the entire data lifecycle. The study aims to provide a structured framework for cloud storage providers to establish a level of effective privacy, ensuring the protection of sensitive data and compliance with regulatory requirements.
