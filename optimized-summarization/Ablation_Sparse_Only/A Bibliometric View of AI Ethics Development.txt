The research paper discusses the development of AI Ethics over the last 20 years, identifying three phases: incubation, making AI human-like machines, and making AI human-centric machines. It suggests that the next phase will focus on making AI more machine-like as AI matches or surpasses humans intellectually. The paper emphasizes the importance of AI ethicists addressing ethical concerns related to AI development, such as preventing unethical AI development and ensuring responsible AI deployment. It also highlights the role of keywords in tracking the evolution of AI Ethics literature and the need for proactive measures to align AI development with ethical standards. The study contributes to ongoing discussions on AI Ethics and provides insights into the future direction of AI development.
