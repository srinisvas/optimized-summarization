The paper introduces CenterYou++, a hybrid privacy enforcement framework for Android environments that combines lightweight on-device AI with cloud-based decision support. It addresses privacy challenges by providing real-time privacy enforcement, user-centric design, and scalability. The framework's architecture is described, and its advantages over existing solutions are highlighted. Future research directions include federated learning and blockchain integration. Key phrases include privacy, privacy enforcement, on-device AI, cloud, user privacy, and hybrid framework. The paper emphasizes the importance of balancing privacy protection with user experience and scalability.
