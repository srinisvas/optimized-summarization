Executive Summary
The proliferation of Big Data and the consequential shift towards cloud computing services, which offer unlimited storage capabilities, easy access, and decreased costs, necessitate robust solutions for data security. While organizations prefer encrypting sensitive information—such as medical records or financial details—before outsourcing it to remote cloud servers to mitigate the risk of data breaches, this encryption significantly hinders the server’s ability to perform efficient plaintext keyword searches, thereby affecting data retrieval. This conflict between privacy protection and data utility has led to the development of various privacy-preserving keyword search (PPKS) schemes, a core cryptographic primitive widely known as searchable encryption.

This paper provides an extensive analysis and survey of recently proposed PPKS techniques specifically designed to mitigate privacy issues related to encrypted cloud data. The methodology involved a detailed comparative study focusing on key requirements such as verifiability, efficiency, and data privacy to highlight the advantages and limitations of prominent schemes. A key finding from the analysis indicated a gap in current approaches: only one of the compared schemes incorporated a verifiability technique, which is crucial because it allows for the detection of attackers who steal secret keys and perform illegal searches. Furthermore, among the summarized schemes, only two utilized a multiple data owner system model, which enables authenticated data users to issue queries without possessing the secret keys of different data owners.

Ultimately, the analysis serves to assist readers in understanding the specific design goals required for PPKS techniques, particularly in terms of security and efficiency. Through this comprehensive evaluation of existing keyword search solutions, the paper concludes by presenting valuable directions for future research. Specifically, future work should concentrate on designing an efficient privacy-preserving ranked fuzzy keyword search scheme, ensuring it meets crucial requirements including verifiability, security, efficiency, and dynamic search capabilities.
