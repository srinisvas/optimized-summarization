Executive Summary
This article investigates the complex ethical, privacy, and security challenges arising from integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) and blockchain technologies within digital health ecosystems, aiming to compare and evaluate their potential contributions toward fostering honest and ethical healthcare choices. The research examines several cutting-edge privacy-preserving techniques, including federated learning, homomorphic encryption, differential privacy, zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs), self-sovereign identity (SSI) solutions, and explainable AI systems. While these approaches were rated based on data protection, transparency, and system security, the analysis determined that each technique possesses inherent merits and downsides, confirming that while approaches like ZKPs allow one party to prove a point without disclosing underlying facts and SSI lets individuals own their data, these individual issues may render them collectively unsuitable for comprehensive application in healthcare, especially since explainable models may slow down system growth. Blockchain, however, uniquely enhances the specificity of access permissions, empowering patients to understand how their data is used.

To address the major issues remaining in existing techniques, the paper proposes a novel solution that not only outperforms existing speed standards but also continuously improves ethical decision-making through dynamic risk assessment. This system evaluates the trustworthiness and responsibility of outcomes by actively calculating a total ethical risk score derived from combining individual scores for bias, fairness, and transparency, weighted by predetermined coefficients. Decision rules are established for evaluation, ensuring the AI system is prevented from selecting winners and losers based on demographic factors. If the predicted probability derived from an alternate model exceeds a defined threshold, the decision is flagged, and revised risks are reviewed to determine if disclosure is required, thereby providing an organized and transparent approach to handling ethical dilemmas.

The application of this framework demonstrates that integrating dynamic risk assessment and decision confirmation within a federated approach ensures that AI and blockchain technologies in healthcare are fair, impartial, and transparent. By focusing on the continuous improvement of ethical decision-making, these evaluations enhance the reliability, fairness, and clarity of AI systems during critical decision-making processes, resulting in trustworthy and responsible outcomes that are socially accountable and government compliant. The ultimate conclusion is that cutting-edge solutions and moral creativity can effectively combine privacy, ethics, and security, maximizing the potential of AI and blockchain to enhance health outcomes while maintaining justice, thus paving the way for a robust and moral healthcare paradigm.
