**Executive Summary**

This paper introduces a novel technique designed to secure private data within AI-powered medical systems while simultaneously ensuring effective data analysis and insights. Recognizing the necessity of robust privacy protection in healthcare, the proposed method integrates complex mechanisms, including training group models, managing secure data, and specifically incorporating Laplace distribution noise, all guided by a privacy budget that balances individual contributions against overall analysis performance. The foundational architecture utilizes federated learning and blockchain technology for secure data exchange, a strategy deemed superior to centralizing patient data, thereby boosting AI performance while preserving privacy.

The implementation of this solution incorporates several specialized security protocols. For secure clinical research, homomorphic cryptography is employed as an effective means for transmitting medical data across different organizations or nations. Differential privacy concepts and zero-knowledge proof are utilized, alongside data anonymization, which provides highly useful data quickly, making it a good option for fast data access 92% of the time. Resilience is further increased through the use of regularization techniques like stochastic gradient descent. The process involves selecting and estimating sensitive data first, followed by necessary output modifications to the dataset, culminating in a finalized global model ready for deployment following a privacy audit to ensure regulatory compliance, such as with GDPR and HIPAA.

The results confirm that this new approach outperforms existing methodologies across key metrics, including accuracy, precision, memory, F1 score, and privacy compliance. The F1 score, which displays the balance between precision and recall, indicates the overall reliability of the technique. Furthermore, performance enhancements are achieved in data, models, training, and inference speeds, making the system highly suitable for real-time healthcare applications. An improving AUC-ROC curve confirms the model's enhanced diagnostic capabilities. This balanced design combines excellent analytical abilities with rigorous privacy precautions, enabling AI-powered medical systems to identify and anticipate findings, thereby setting a new, scalable standard for AI application privacy that enhances decision-making and patient outcomes.
