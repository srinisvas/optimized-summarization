**Executive Summary**

The proliferation of digital patient data for scientific and analytical purposes requires sophisticated security measures, yet ensuring the confidentiality, availability, and sustained compliance with frameworks like HIPAA and GDPR presents a major challenge, as traditional cryptographic methods are often static, inefficient, and resource-intensive. This research proposes a novel AI-driven approach to enhance healthcare data security and privacy by integrating machine learning algorithms with existing encryption techniques to create a dynamic, context-adaptable security environment. Use of AI in encryption assures that data is secured precisely to the level required, depending on the nature of the data, the location where it is accessed, and the identity of the user, while also enabling the systems to learn from previous incidents and continuously improve their enhanced detection mechanisms against new hazards.

The methodology strategically incorporates privacy-preserving techniques, including differential privacy, federated learning, and homomorphic encryption, to facilitate secure collaboration in the analysis and research of patient data while maintaining privacy. For instance, differential privacy adds noise to the data to prevent the retrieval of specific records, and federated learning restricts data transfers, reducing privacy intrusiveness; however, while homomorphic encryption offers a very high level of security, its inherent complexity currently results in significantly high computational cost and memory requirements. Nonetheless, recent design efforts focusing on AI-aided methods have been successful in mitigating some of these drawbacks, providing better flexibility and more effective computations by adjusting cryptographic key parameters based on data patterns.

Evaluation results confirm that the proposed AI-based encryption model achieves superior security outcomes and lower computational time complexity compared to common methods, demonstrating a robust performance with a very low false violation rate (1.2%). Furthermore, the framework integrates compliance management, automating regulatory tasks and significantly reducing the time-consuming duties and workload for healthcare organizations, thus ensuring sustained adherence to international paradigms of privacy laws. This methodology provides a comprehensive, scalable, and end-to-end solution for protecting valuable patient information, with future work planned to focus on transitioning the encryption system to quantum-safe algorithms and developing lighter AI models suitable for mHealth and IoT device-driven healthcare systems.
