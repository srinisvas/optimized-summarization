Executive Summary
The rapid progression of Generative AI (GenAI), specifically Foundation Models which generate diverse content forms like text, audio, and visuals, has introduced significant technical and legal challenges concerning data privacy and copyright protection. These concerns stem from the necessity of training GenAI models on vast datasets, yet traditional approaches like differential privacy or machine unlearning offer only fragmented solutions to these complex issues. This paper advocates for integrated approaches informed by a comprehensive data lifecycle perspective, aiming to bridge the gap between technical innovation and legal responsibility by ensuring that the usage of data and the downstream distribution of models and derivative data are legally compliant.

These interwoven challenges are continuous, spanning multiple stages, primarily associated with Data Collection and Model Training. During the data collection phase, the obscurity of data harvesting often leaves individuals oblivious to the use of their personal or copyrighted material, complicating the enforcement of key rights, including the Right to be Informed, Access, Rectification, and Erasure, as well as the six exclusive rights protected under Title 17 of the US Copyright Law. Identifying such unauthorized uses is challenging without enhanced transparency mechanisms, and even when licenses exist, failure to properly attribute data constitutes a legal hurdle leading to copyright infringement. While GenAI companies currently deploy rudimentary safeguards, such as keyword filters or prompt prefixes, these challenges become more intricate and difficult to manage as the process progresses through model training and deployment.

Achieving reliable guardrails for privacy and copyright protection necessitates a comprehensive and coordinated effort throughout the entire GenAI data lifecycle. The investigation maps these challenges and advocates for further research into solutions informed by a lifecycle perspective, including areas like Machine Forgetting and Side Effects Mitigation. The overall goal is to create a robust and adaptive system that can effectively respond to the ever-changing challenges posed by GenAI, thereby safeguarding privacy and copyright, emphasizing that such vigilance is essential to ensure protections keep pace with the advancements and novel exploitation methods in this field.
