Executive Summary
The present paper addresses the critical and ongoing problem of user data privacy invasion and digital data loss within cloud storage area services, a concern exacerbated by the ease with which anonymous users and unknown hackers can access confidential government and private sector information. Recognizing that conventional protective measures are inadequate against this backdrop of persistent technological advancement, the research introduces a novel, layered framework designed to mitigate privacy risk: the Onion and Garlic Privacy Preserving Cohesive Approach (OGPPA). This proposed framework is specifically engineered to preserve the secrecy of cloud user data and provide robust protection against data breaches.

The OGPPA achieves security through multiple layers of encryption and obfuscation techniques. Messages are repeatedly encrypted, mirroring concepts found in onion routing, and the framework utilizes a process of multiple encryption across the Onion and Garlic layers to significantly increase the difficulty for attackers attempting to interrupt or intercept confidential information. The proposed system converts user data via onion encryption before storage in the cloud environment. Furthermore, each layer of the OGPPA is configured to perform a certain time variation on the request before transmitting it to the next layer, adding complexity to the overall security posture and preventing attackers from establishing patterns in the data flow.

The Onion and Garlic privacy preserving framework provides a strong backup through its combined encryption and decryption capabilities, effectively handling privacy breaches that occur during critical attacks by unauthorized users and ensuring that protected user data is transferred only to original, authorized individuals. While the framework successfully mitigates privacy risks, the authors note a current limitation regarding its cost-effectiveness, suggesting that implementation may be expensive in fields where privacy is essential. Consequently, future research efforts are planned to focus on implementing three-factor encryption within the cloud layer to further boost mitigation and prevention of privacy compromise in cloud storage services.
