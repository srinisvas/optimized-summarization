**Executive Summary**

The impressive advancements of Artificial Intelligence, particularly within the Computer Vision (CV) sub-field—encompassing functions like detection, recognition, classification, prediction, and image and video generation—necessitate a consideration of its ethical impact on human rights, sustainable development, and the planet. This paper analyzes the extent to which recent CV research aligns with the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), which are assumed as references for ethical development targets, covering a temporal window ranging from 2018 to 2022. The investigation included an analysis of 3282 papers in the scope of the SDGs presented at both ECCV and CVPR during this period, generating data that serves as a clue regarding CV’s potential contribution to SDG achievement.

The analysis indicates that the distribution of CV papers across the SDGs is similar to the general distribution observed for AI papers. However, it specifically highlights that CV can play a pivotal role, particularly in environment-related SDG targets, where it shows a higher relative impact compared to broader AI applications focused on Society or Economy. Major projected contributions emerge in SDG11 (supporting accessible and sustainable transport systems and promoting inclusive urbanization), SDG9 (promoting sustainable industrialization and improving production methods), and SDG3 (supporting health solutions for diagnosis, prognosis, and prevention). Conversely, the investigation found no impactful papers related to SDG1, SDG5, and SDG17, while other goals showed low impact, including SDG16 (4.27%), SDG4 (2.10%), SDG8 (1.62%), SDG7 (1.43%), and SDG10 (0.67%).

Although the research reveals the extent of coincidence between the scope of Computer Vision research and the scope of the SDGs, suggesting its potential to contribute critically to these goals, the findings indicate a low sensitivity within the CV research community regarding this potential impact. This conclusion is supported by the observation that in the large majority of cases, authors do not mention the SDGs among the motivation of their research. This gap suggests that disseminating information on the SDGs and their targets is an essential task for Computer Vision and AI Ethics, serving to promote greater awareness of sustainable ethics and guide these technologies toward improving the well-being of both human beings and the planet's ecosystem.
