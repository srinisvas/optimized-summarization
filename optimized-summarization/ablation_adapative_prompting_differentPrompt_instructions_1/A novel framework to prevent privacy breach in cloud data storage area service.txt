**Executive Summary**

This paper introduces a novel framework to prevent privacy breaches in cloud data storage area services. The proposed approach combines onion and garlic privacy preserving layers, which are designed to protect user data from unauthorized access and digital data loss. Onion privacy preserving approach encrypts user data using public key cryptography, while garlic privacy encrypts multiple messages together to make it more difficult for attackers to intercept and analyze user requests. The framework is layered, with onion and garlic privacy layers on top of the cloud layer, and virtualization management for efficient data storage and retrieval. The proposed system uses encryption and decryption techniques to secure user data, ensuring that only authorized users can access it. Future research will focus on integrating three-factor encryption in the cloud layer to further enhance privacy protection.
