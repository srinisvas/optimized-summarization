**Executive Summary**

This paper addresses the multifaceted nature of artificial intelligence (AI) and its ethical implications. It proposes an anthropocentric approach to AI ethics, focusing on both subject-object moral considerations to provide a comprehensive understanding of AI's ethical landscape. The study acknowledges the anthropocentric approach's limitations and suggests that it can help describe the spirit of AI ethics principles by analyzing both subject and object moral perspectives. The paper aims to provide a balanced view of AI ethics, emphasizing the importance of considering both human-centric and non-human-centric aspects.
