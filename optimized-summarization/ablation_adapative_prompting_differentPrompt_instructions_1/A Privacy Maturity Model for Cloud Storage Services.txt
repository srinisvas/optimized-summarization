**Executive Summary**

This paper introduces a Privacy Maturity Model for Cloud Storage Services (PMMCS), aiming to assess the privacy afforded by cloud storage providers. The model is based on the CMMI-SVC framework and the BSIMM-CP model, focusing on compliance policies and governance related to Personally Identifiable Information (PII). The authors propose a four-phase methodology for developing and evaluating the proposed solution: Planning and Systematic Review, Development of the Solution, Evaluation of the Solution, and Publication of Results. The PMMCS aims to address privacy concerns in cloud storage by providing a structured approach to ensure privacy compliance, balancing user needs with service provider responsibilities.
