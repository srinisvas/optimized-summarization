**Executive Summary**

This paper explores the ethical, privacy, and security challenges posed by integrating AI and blockchain technologies in digital health ecosystems. It evaluates various techniques such as federated learning, homomorphic encryption, differential privacy, zero-knowledge proofs, self-sovereign identity systems, explainable AI, blockchain interface protocols, and privacypreserving AI systems. The analysis focuses on data protection, ethical data collection, fairness, transparency, and system security. While most approaches show promise in certain areas, they face limitations that may hinder their adoption in healthcare. The proposed solution addresses these concerns by combining ethical principles with technical safeguards, improving healthcare AI systems' reliability, fairness, and clarity during decision-making.

The paper introduces an Ethical Risk Scoring Algorithm (ERSA) to assess AI-driven healthcare decisions based on bias, fairness, and transparency. The algorithm evaluates the ethical risk score by combining individual scores for bias, fairness, and transparency, weighted by predefined factors. This approach helps in identifying and mitigating ethical risks in AI-driven healthcare systems. The methodology also involves dynamic risk assessment and decision confirmation to ensure ethical compliance and fairness in healthcare AI applications.

The paper concludes that AI and blockchain technologies have the potential to transform digital health ecosystems, but their successful integration requires addressing ethical, privacy, and security challenges. By combining privacy-preserving techniques with ethical frameworks, it is possible to create robust, fair, and transparent healthcare AI systems that comply with regulations and prioritize patient welfare.
