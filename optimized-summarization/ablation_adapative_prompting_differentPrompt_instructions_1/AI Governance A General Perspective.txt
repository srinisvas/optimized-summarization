**Executive Summary**

This paper discusses the importance of AI governance, addressing the risks and challenges posed by artificial intelligence in various sectors such as healthcare, education, business, and transportation. Key risks include privacy breaches, discrimination, economic disruption, security threats, accountability issues, ethical concerns, regulatory gaps, and environmental impacts. The paper highlights the need for comprehensive AI governance frameworks that incorporate principles such as accountability, transparency, fairness, data privacy, ethical use, inclusivity, sustainability, adaptability, collaboration, and human oversight.

The paper reviews various international initiatives and principles of AI governance, including the Shanghai Declaration on Global AI Governance, the IMF Framework for AI Governance, UNESCO's AI Ethics Recommendation, the OECD AI Principles, and the European AI Act. These initiatives emphasize the importance of ethical, transparent, and fair AI development, with a focus on ensuring AI benefits humanity while minimizing risks.

The paper concludes by advocating for the development of AI governance frameworks that foster international cooperation, adapt to rapid technological changes, and prioritize human oversight. It suggests holding an international conference under the UN's auspices to develop common guidelines for responsible AI use. The paper also calls for further research into AI governance frameworks, particularly in areas such as privacy protection, transparency, and inclusivity.
