**Executive Summary**

This paper examines the development of AI systems and their ethical implications, leading to the emergence of AI Ethics as a distinct field. It analyzes existing ethical documents in the field of AI, categorizing them based on their status, sources, and moral agency. The documents are grouped into proactive ethical codes, those from intergovernmental organizations, recommendations from state authorities, professional organizations, companies, and corporate ethical codes.

The paper highlights the challenges in defining AI Ethics, as it is a broad field encompassing various technologies like machine learning and deep learning. The ethical issues in AI are primarily concerned with regulating human relationships and behavior, similar to other technology ethics. The review of the documents reveals that they often focus on common principles such as fairness, non-discrimination, responsibility, respect for human rights, and accountability.

Key ethical concepts discussed in the documents include transparency, privacy, and trust. Transparency refers to the ability of AI systems to provide clear explanations for their decisions, which is crucial for building trust and ensuring accountability. Privacy is a significant concern, especially with the increasing use of digital technologies and AI, as it involves the protection of personal data. Trust is central to the ethical discourse, as it affects people's willingness to accept and use AI technologies.

The paper concludes that while the reviewed documents have identified common ethical issues and principles, they lack practical recommendations for their implementation. The main goal of these documents is to improve the application of AI technologies in daily life and to increase trust among users. The authors argue that the ethical documents have largely completed the stage of identifying key issues and concepts in AI Ethics, though there is some disagreement about their interpretations. The practical application of these principles remains a significant challenge.
