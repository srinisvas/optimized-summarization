**Executive Summary**

This paper examines the ethical and legal implications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in business and employment contexts, focusing on privacy, bias, and accountability. Drawing on a comprehensive literature review, it proposes a multifaceted framework to address privacy concerns, combat algorithmic bias, and establish accountability mechanisms. The framework integrates advanced encryption for privacy preservation, bias-detection algorithms for fairness, and transparent decision-making processes to enhance accountability. The proposed model, supported by quantitative and qualitative analyses, demonstrates significant advancements in each domain, providing a foundation for responsible AI deployment. The results show robust privacy protection, accurate bias detection, and comprehensible explanations, contributing to the ongoing discourse on ethical AI by offering practical solutions to address the evolving challenges. The framework aims to balance the benefits of AI technologies with ethical considerations, ensuring alignment with societal values and legal standards.
