**Executive Summary**

This paper addresses the privacy concerns associated with cloud computing, a rapidly growing technology in the IT sector. It outlines various privacy issues that arise when data is processed in the cloud, including compliance, access, storage, retention, audit, and breach notification. The study also examines existing privacy frameworks from different countries and identifies key privacy principles that should be considered in cloud computing. The framework proposed in the paper aims to provide a comprehensive approach to addressing privacy issues in cloud computing, ensuring that cloud service providers comply with relevant laws and regulations while maintaining transparency and accountability. The paper concludes by emphasizing the importance of transparency and accountability in cloud computing and suggests future work on developing new frameworks that integrate perspectives from various privacy frameworks.
