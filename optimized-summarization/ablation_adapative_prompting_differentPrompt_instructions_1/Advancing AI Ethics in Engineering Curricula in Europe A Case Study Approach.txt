**Executive Summary**

The Erasmus+ Ethical Engineer project aims to enhance AI education by integrating ethical, social, and legal aspects into engineering training, promoting truthful AI in Europe. The paper presents a structured approach for developing case studies that address ethical issues in AI applications. Key case studies include AI in healthcare, witness testimonies, facial recognition, self-driving taxis, generative AI's ecological impact, AI in marketing, assessing historic photos, continuous artificial hearts, and AI in data analytics. Each case study is designed to foster critical thinking, ethical reasoning, and awareness of the broader social, political, economic, and ethical implications of AI. The case studies are structured to present scenarios, background information, ethical dilemmas, diverse perspectives, and open-ended questions, with recommendations for teaching activities such as group discussions, role-playing, reflective writing, and guest speaker sessions. The project's methodology involves testing and refining the case studies based on feedback from teachers and students, aiming to prepare future engineers better to navigate ethical dilemmas and uphold professional integrity in their careers.
