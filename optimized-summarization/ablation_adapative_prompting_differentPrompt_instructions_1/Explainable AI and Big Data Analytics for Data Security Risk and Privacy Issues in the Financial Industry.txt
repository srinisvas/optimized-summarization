**Executive Summary**

This paper reviews the current literature on the importance of data security in the financial industry when using Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) and Big Data Analytics. It highlights the challenges of secure data processing and privacy concerns in the financial sector, particularly in the context of XAI and big data analytics. The paper emphasizes the need for comprehensive strategies to maintain data privacy and security, especially as the financial industry increasingly relies on automated decision-making processes.

The paper introduces a framework that integrates XAI with big data analytics to enhance data security and privacy in financial institutions. It also proposes a new risk assessment model that leverages XAI to identify and mitigate potential security threats in financial data systems. Additionally, the paper presents novel privacy-preserving techniques that utilize XAI to protect sensitive financial information while maintaining data utility.

The research aims to provide insights into the best ways to conduct data analytics in a privacy-conscious world and to ensure that data analytics can be conducted with the help of XAI, maximizing efficiency of automated data processing and providing results with explanations for the reasons behind the outcomes. The paper contributes to the existing literature on data analytics with AI and provides analysis for the financial industry to improve its data analytics capabilities, addressing cost-effectiveness, privacy concerns, and efficiency in data model selection.

The paper also highlights the need for secure artificial intelligence and data analysis systems in the financial industry, focusing on fairness, effectiveness, and transparency in decision-making processes. It underscores the importance of understanding and improving black-box AI systems through XAI techniques to prevent exploitation of AI algorithms.

The paper concludes by emphasizing the necessity for continuous research to innovate and adapt methodologies ensuring the reliability, accuracy, transparency, and security of data analytics with XAI in the dynamic landscape of the financial industry. The research efforts collectively contribute to the evolution of technologies and strategies that mitigate current challenges and anticipate future complexities in the data-driven financial domain.
