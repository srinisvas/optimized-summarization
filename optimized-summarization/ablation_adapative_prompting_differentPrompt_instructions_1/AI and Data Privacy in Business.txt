**Executive Summary**

This review paper examines the intersection of artificial intelligence (AI) and data privacy in modern business applications. It highlights the ethical concerns and privacy risks associated with AI-driven data processing and emphasizes the importance of regulatory compliance and data privacy principles. The paper discusses privacy-focused principles like Privacy by Design, Data Governance, and Transparency, which are necessary to mitigate risks posed by AI. It also analyzes global regulatory environments and the need for data integrity and accountability in AI deployment. The paper proposes a comprehensive approach to building an accountable AI culture, aligning technical progress with privacy ethics and responsible innovation. Key AI technologies, such as machine learning, natural language processing, and robotic process automation, are discussed in the context of data privacy. The paper addresses challenges at the intersection of AI and data privacy, including data minimization vs. data utility, informed consent, international use of AI, and emerging technologies like facial recognition and behavioral analytics. Ethical considerations in AI deployment, including user sovereignty, accountability, transparency, and fairness, are also explored. The paper concludes that proactive privacy strategies, such as Privacy by Design, are essential for ensuring compliance and fostering trust in AI-driven business operations.
