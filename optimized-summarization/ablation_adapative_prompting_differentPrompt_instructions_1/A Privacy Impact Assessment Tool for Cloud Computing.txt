**Executive Summary**

This paper introduces a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) decision support tool designed for cloud computing environments. The tool aims to address privacy and security concerns in cloud computing by systematically evaluating potential privacy risks and compliance issues. It is structured as a knowledge-based system (KBS) that integrates privacy rules from various jurisdictions, enabling it to assess privacy risks and generate tailored reports for organizations.

The tool's architecture includes a knowledge base populated by privacy experts, which is updated regularly. It utilizes a rules engine, such as Drools, to process and analyze user input, generating reports that highlight privacy risks, compliance issues, and necessary actions. The tool is designed to be deployed in a hybrid cloud environment, leveraging private cloud storage for sensitive data and public cloud services for infrastructure and general computing.

The PIA tool is based on a decision support system (DSS) approach, which has been explored in various contexts such as clinical decision applications and life science enterprise solutions. It differs from existing solutions like Microsoft Excel or Apple Numbers by incorporating intelligence into the solution, allowing it to tailor questionnaires and generate reports efficiently. The tool's design aligns with the principles of accountability in cloud computing, emphasizing the use of regulatory, procedural, and technical measures to ensure privacy compliance.

The tool's development is in its conceptual stage, with future steps focusing on integrating stakeholder analysis and workflow, conducting empirical research to populate the knowledge base, exploring AI methods for report generation, and selecting a suitable cloud storage gateway provider. The PIA tool is expected to play a significant role in enhancing privacy in cloud computing environments as adoption of cloud services continues to grow.
