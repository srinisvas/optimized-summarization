**Executive Summary**

This paper discusses the ethical considerations of integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Autonomous and Intelligent Systems (A/IS) into the Internet of Things (IoT). It emphasizes the need for open discussions with society about AI projects, particularly those involving IoT devices, to foster trust and avoid misinformation. The paper references the IEEE Ethically Aligned Design (EAD) framework, which outlines three key pillars: Universal Human Values, Political Self-Determination and Data Agency, and Technical Dependability. These pillars guide the development of AI and IoT technologies to ensure they align with human values and ethical principles, promoting human well-being and environmental sustainability. The paper also highlights the importance of transparency, accountability, and competence in AI development and operation, as well as the need for awareness of misuse and the establishment of regulatory frameworks. It advocates for a collaborative approach involving society, policymakers, and technologists to ensure the responsible and ethical advancement of AI and IoT technologies.
