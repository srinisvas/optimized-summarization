**Executive Summary**

This paper provides an overview of privacy preserving schemes for industrial Internet of Things (IoT). It discusses the challenges of maintaining privacy in industrial IoT systems, which involve digitization of manufacturing, the integration of intelligent technology, and the need for secure data sharing. The paper reviews existing architectures for industrial IoT, including cloud-based and edge-based systems, and identifies privacy issues associated with these architectures.

The authors propose a comprehensive analysis of privacy challenges and solutions in both cloud-based and edge-based industrial IoT systems. They categorize privacy issues into data privacy, location privacy, query method privacy, and identity privacy. They also evaluate the computational complexity and privacy performance of various privacy preserving solutions, including data perturbation, data encryption, and publishing restrictions.

Key contributions of the paper include:

1. **Privacy Issues Analysis**: The paper thoroughly examines privacy issues in industrial IoT systems, including data privacy, location privacy, query method privacy, and identity privacy. It highlights the vulnerabilities of industrial IoT systems to privacy leakage, especially in cloud-based and edge-based architectures.

2. **Privacy Preserving Solutions**: The paper reviews a range of privacy preserving solutions, such as data perturbation, data encryption, and publishing restrictions, and evaluates their effectiveness in addressing privacy issues. It also discusses the computational complexity and privacy performance of these solutions.

3. **Open Issues**: The paper identifies several open research issues, including the need for privacy preserving algorithms that can handle energy constraints in edge-based industrial IoT systems, the challenges of integrating 5G technologies with industrial IoT systems, and the need for further research on privacy preserving schemes for industrial IoT systems.

4. **Conclusion**: The paper concludes by summarizing the key findings and open issues, emphasizing the importance of addressing privacy challenges in industrial IoT systems to ensure the security and integrity of data and the confidentiality of user information. It serves as a valuable reference for further research and development in the field of industrial IoT privacy.
