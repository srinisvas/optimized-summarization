**Executive Summary**

This study examines the ethical considerations surrounding the use of artificial intelligence (AI) in university education. The literature review highlights key ethical dimensions, including the preservation of academic integrity, concerns about misleading and low-quality AI outputs, the potential displacement of educators, data privacy, and the impact on fundamental human rights. The integration of AI tools in educational settings raises questions about the ethical implications of their use, particularly in terms of maintaining academic honesty, ensuring the quality of AI-generated outputs, and balancing the role of human educators with automated processes. The study emphasizes the need for clear ethical guidelines, robust oversight mechanisms, and training programs to address these ethical concerns and promote responsible AI integration in higher education.
