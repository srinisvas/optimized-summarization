**Executive Summary**

This paper introduces CenterYou++, a novel hybrid privacy enforcement framework for Android devices. CenterYou++ combines lightweight on-device artificial intelligence with cloud-based decision support to enhance privacy protection. The framework leverages on-device AI to perform real-time privacy enforcement, reducing reliance on cloud communication and improving system responsiveness. It also includes a cloud-based decision engine for policy administration and threat intelligence. The hybrid architecture aims to balance privacy protection with user convenience, scalability, and real-time responsiveness. The paper highlights the framework's benefits, such as low-latency privacy enforcement, reduced bandwidth consumption, and offline resilience, while also acknowledging limitations like initial network connectivity and energy requirements. Future work includes integrating federated learning for privacy-preserving model updates and exploring blockchain for policy management.
