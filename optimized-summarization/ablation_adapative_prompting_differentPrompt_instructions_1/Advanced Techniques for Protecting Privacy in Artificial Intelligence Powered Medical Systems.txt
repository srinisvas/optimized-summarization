**Executive Summary**

This research paper presents a novel technique to protect privacy in AI-powered medical systems, addressing the critical challenge of balancing data utility and individual privacy. The proposed method integrates differential privacy, homomorphic encryption, and federated learning to safeguard patient data while enabling effective data analysis and model training. The approach uses Laplace distribution noise to manage privacy budgets, ensuring that individual contributions remain confidential. The framework incorporates iterative feedback mechanisms to refine the model based on real-world data, enhancing its adaptability and performance.

Key contributions include:

1. **Privacy Budget Management**: A Laplace distribution noise mechanism is employed to manage privacy budgets, ensuring that individual data points remain private while maintaining the utility of the data for AI analysis.

2. **Federated Learning**: This distributed learning approach allows healthcare providers to contribute to an AI model without sharing sensitive patient data, promoting collaboration and data security.

3. **Homomorphic Encryption**: The use of homomorphic encryption enables AI systems to process encrypted data, preventing the leakage of sensitive information during analysis.

4. **Sensitivity Analysis**: The method includes a sensitivity analysis to determine the impact of individual data points on the overall model, allowing for more precise privacy budget management.

5. **Robustness and Regularization**: The framework incorporates regularization techniques to prevent overfitting and ensure the model's robustness, especially when dealing with varied datasets.

The proposed technique demonstrates significant improvements in accuracy, precision, and F1 score compared to existing methods, while also maintaining high privacy compliance. The approach reduces computational overhead and improves data processing speed, making it suitable for real-time healthcare applications. The use of feedback mechanisms and iterative refinement further enhances the model's adaptability and performance, ensuring that AI-driven medical systems can provide both effective analysis and robust privacy protection.
