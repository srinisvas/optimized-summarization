**Executive Summary**

This paper explores the intersection of GDPR and AI, focusing on the protection of human rights in the context of digital media systems. It highlights the differences between GDPR and AI in terms of privacy, data usage, and individual rights. GDPR emphasizes transparency, data minimization, and accountability, while AI lacks clear regulatory guidelines and mechanisms for individual protection. The paper discusses the ethical implications of AI, such as the potential for misinformation and the erosion of privacy, and proposes the need for a unified legal framework to address these concerns. It also emphasizes the importance of transparency and accountability in AI development to ensure respect for human rights.
