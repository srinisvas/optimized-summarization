**Executive Summary**

This paper introduces AI-generated privacy-preserving protocols for cross-cloud data sharing and collaboration. The proposed solution integrates federated learning, differential privacy, dynamic encryption, and context-aware policies to enhance data security and privacy across heterogeneous cloud environments. The architecture includes a Data Ingestion Layer, AI Processing Module, Secured Data Sharing Layer, and Cross-Cloud Collaboration Layer. Experimental simulations in a simulated multicloud environment demonstrate improved privacy scores, higher attack resistance, and lower latency compared to existing protocols like SMC and ABE. The proposed protocols balance privacy protection with performance efficiency, with metrics such as privacy, security, and performance evaluated. Future work includes optimizing resource management, improving model aggregation, and exploring quantum-resistant protocols and blockchain integration to further enhance the security and efficiency of cross-cloud data sharing.
