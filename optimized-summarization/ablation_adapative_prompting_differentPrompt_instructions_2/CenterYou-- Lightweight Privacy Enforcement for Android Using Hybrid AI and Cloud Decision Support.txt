**Executive Summary**

This research introduces CenterYou++, a new privacy enforcement framework for Android devices that combines lightweight on-device artificial intelligence with cloud-based decision support. The framework aims to protect user privacy while maintaining a user-friendly experience. On-device AI agents handle real-time privacy enforcement, using learned behavioral patterns and predefined rules. The cloud component provides access to current privacy policies and threat intelligence, ensuring scalability. CenterYou++ addresses the limitations of previous cloud-only systems by performing initial privacy assessments and enforcement actions locally on the device, reducing reliance on cloud communication and improving system responsiveness. The hybrid architecture balances robust privacy protection with efficient resource usage, offering low-latency privacy enforcement, a more intuitive user experience, and strengthened policy enforcement. Future work includes integrating federated learning for privacy-preserving model updates and exploring blockchain for secure policy management.
