**Executive Summary**

This research paper talks about how to keep data private when sharing it across different cloud platforms. It uses artificial intelligence (AI) to create new ways to protect data while still allowing people to work together on projects. The main idea is to use AI to find weaknesses in data security, add extra protection when needed, and make sure everyone follows privacy rules.

The paper describes a system that uses AI to help with three main tasks: finding and fixing security problems, adding extra privacy measures based on what the data is about, and making sure everyone follows the same privacy rules. They tested this system on a pretend multi-cloud environment and found it worked better than other methods at keeping data safe and sharing it efficiently.

The authors also suggest ways to make the system even better, like using more advanced AI techniques and looking into new technologies like quantum-resistant methods. They hope that their work will help make data sharing safer and more efficient in the future.
