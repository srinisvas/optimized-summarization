**Executive Summary**

This paper talks about how to keep people's private information safe when using the cloud. The cloud is like a big storage room where lots of data is kept, and many people can access it. But, if someone gets into the cloud, they might see or use people's private information without their permission.

The paper suggests a new way to keep people's private information safe. It uses something called "onion" and "garlic" layers to encrypt (hide) the data. This way, even if someone tries to get into the cloud, they can't easily find the private information.

The authors tested their new method and found it works well. They also think that this method can be used in many places where people need to keep their information private, like on the internet or in smart devices.

So, the main idea of the paper is to help keep people's private information safe when they use the cloud, by using special layers to encrypt their data.
