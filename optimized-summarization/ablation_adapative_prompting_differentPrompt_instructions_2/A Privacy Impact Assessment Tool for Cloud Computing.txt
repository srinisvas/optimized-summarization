**Executive Summary**

This paper introduces a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) decision support tool designed for cloud computing environments. The tool helps organizations assess privacy risks and compliance issues in their projects, providing actionable insights to ensure privacy protection. It is structured as a web-based service accessible via a browser, allowing users to input project details and receive a comprehensive report highlighting potential privacy risks, compliance issues, and required actions.

The tool uses a knowledge base (KB) populated by privacy experts, which is updated regularly. It employs a rules engine (Drools) to generate questionnaires tailored to specific contexts and analyze privacy risks, grouping them into categories such as transborder data flows, compliance with legislation, and security. The output is a report with visual indicators for risk levels, detailed analysis, and a checklist of actions to mitigate risks.

The paper discusses related work in privacy and security in cloud computing, highlighting the need for accountability and compliance in cloud environments. It also presents a novel approach to cloud-based privacy impact assessments, emphasizing the tool's ability to balance security and functionality, and its potential to enhance end-user trust and organizational privacy standards.

Next steps include refining the tool's stakeholder analysis and workflow integration, conducting empirical research for initial rule sets, exploring AI methods for report generation, and selecting a cloud storage gateway provider. The tool aims to provide a proactive and efficient method for organizations to ensure privacy compliance in their cloud projects.
