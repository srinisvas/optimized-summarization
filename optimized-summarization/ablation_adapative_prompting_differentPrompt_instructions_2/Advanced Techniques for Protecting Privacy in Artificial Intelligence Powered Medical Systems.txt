**Executive Summary**

This research paper discusses how to protect privacy in AI-powered medical systems. The main idea is to use special techniques like adding noise to data and using different privacy budgets to keep patient data safe while still allowing useful analysis. The authors tested these methods and found they work well in balancing privacy and performance. They also talk about using different privacy methods like differential privacy and homomorphic encryption to keep data secure. The paper shows that these techniques can improve the accuracy and speed of AI in healthcare, while also ensuring that patient data is protected. The goal is to create a new standard for privacy in AI medical systems that is both secure and efficient.
