**Executive Summary**

This research paper explores the ethical implications of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in healthcare. It delves into the challenges and ethical concerns that arise when AI is integrated into healthcare systems, such as biases, privacy issues, and the need for transparency and accountability. The paper emphasizes the importance of ethical guidelines and frameworks to ensure that AI systems are designed and deployed responsibly. It also highlights the need for human oversight and collaboration between medical practitioners and AI developers to mitigate potential risks and ensure that AI enhances healthcare without compromising patient rights and privacy. The study calls for a patient-centric approach, involving patients and healthcare professionals in the development and evaluation of AI technologies to promote trust and ethical use of AI in healthcare.
