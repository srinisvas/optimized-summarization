**Executive Summary**

This research paper, titled "Advancing AI Ethics in Engineering Curricula in Europe A Case Study Approach," focuses on integrating ethical considerations into engineering education, particularly in the context of artificial intelligence (AI). The Erasmus+ Ethical Engineer project aims to enhance AI education by incorporating ethical, social, and legal aspects into engineering training, promoting truthful AI in Europe.

The paper discusses the importance of ethical instruction in engineering education, emphasizing the need for students to navigate complex ethical dilemmas posed by AI. Traditional engineering ethics education focuses on core principles like safety, honesty, and fairness, but the rapid advancement of AI introduces new challenges. The paper outlines objectives for ethical instruction in engineering education, such as stimulating ethical imagination, teaching students to recognize ethical issues, and improving ethical judgment.

The paper also presents a structured template for developing AI ethics case studies. These case studies are designed to cover various applications of AI, including healthcare, witness testimonies, facial recognition, self-driving taxis, ecological impact, marketing processes, assessing historic photos, continuous artificial hearts, and data analytics. Each case study includes a scenario, background information, ethical considerations, discussion points, and open-ended questions to encourage critical thinking and discussion.

The paper highlights the need for engineering students to develop practical skills like ethical reasoning, critical thinking, and communication. It suggests using case studies, simulations, role-playing, and real-world projects to apply ethical principles in realistic scenarios. The case studies are intended to provide students with a safe environment to explore ethical dilemmas and make informed decisions.

The paper concludes by advocating for the integration of AI ethics into engineering curricula, emphasizing the importance of preparing students for the unique challenges posed by AI. It recommends using the structured template and case studies to enhance ethical instruction in engineering education and promote responsible AI development.
