**Executive Summary**

This research paper discusses the importance of AI governance, which refers to the processes, standards, and barriers that ensure the safety and ethics of AI systems. The paper highlights several key risks associated with AI, including privacy risks, discrimination risks, economic market and job opportunity risks, security risks, accountability and transparency risks, ethical concerns, regulatory gaps, and the need for inclusivity, sustainability, adaptability, collaboration, and human oversight.

The paper emphasizes the necessity of establishing frameworks for AI governance to protect human rights, ensure fairness, and promote the responsible use of AI. It mentions various international initiatives and principles that have been proposed, such as the Shanghai Declaration on Global AI Governance, the IMF Framework for AI Governance, UNESCO's AI Ethics Recommendation, the OECD AI Principles, and the European AI Act.

The paper also discusses the importance of transparency, accountability, fairness, data privacy, ethical use, inclusivity, sustainability, adaptability, collaboration, and human oversight in AI governance. It calls for international cooperation and the development of comprehensive AI governance frameworks that can evolve with technological advancements and address emerging risks.

The paper concludes by advocating for the development of global AI governance frameworks that can be implemented and adapted to various sectors and countries, and encourages further research in this area. It also appreciates the progress made by the European Union in regulating AI at the EU level.
