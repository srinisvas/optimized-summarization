The paper describes the development of a Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA) decision support tool for cloud computing environments. The tool aims to help organizations assess and mitigate privacy risks by providing a systematic process for identifying potential privacy issues. The tool's architecture is based on a knowledge base (KB) created and updated by privacy experts, and a rules engine that generates questionnaires and outputs reports. The tool is designed to be integrated into the cloud environment and can be accessed through a web browser. It covers privacy and security issues in cloud computing, such as data transfers and storage, and includes guidelines for compliance with different jurisdictions. The tool is expected to help organizations comply with privacy regulations and reduce the risk of exposure of end user's information in a cloud environment. The paper also discusses related work on privacy and security in cloud computing and proposes next steps for the development of the PIA tool.}
